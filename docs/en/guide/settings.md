# Settings

The Settings page centralizes personal panel preferences and browser-session account controls.

## Language and direction

Qbit Console supports English/LTR and Persian/RTL at runtime.

## Calendar

Gregorian and Jalali calendars can be selected independently from language. In “follow language default” mode, English defaults to Gregorian and Persian defaults to Jalali. An explicit calendar override survives later language changes.

## Appearance

Light and dark modes are first-class. The interface also respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Account sessions

Settings can show browser-session accounts, switch the active account, remove an additional account, and sign out.

::: tip
After switching accounts, navigate to a Workspace available to the new account. Qbit resets sensitive subject-scoped state, while the browser URL may still reference a route from the previous account.
:::
